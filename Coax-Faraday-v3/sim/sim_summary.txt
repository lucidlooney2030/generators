Coax Faraday v3 simulation summary
=====================================
Magnet grade assumption: N42, Br=1.3 T (N52 Br=1.44 optional)
Geometry: R_inner_face=33.0 mm, coil_radial=9.0 mm, R_outer_face≈46.0 mm

Inner magnet decision candidates:
  outer20_inner20_dual: P=8, Bg=0.311 T, Phi=6.8364e-05 Wb, FoM=P*Phi=5.4691e-04
  outer20_inner3_dual: P=12, Bg=0.311 T, Phi=1.5382e-06 Wb, FoM=P*Phi=1.8458e-05
  outer20_only_single: P=8, Bg=0.144 T, Phi=2.4958e-05 Wb, FoM=P*Phi=1.9967e-04

CHOSEN: Ø20×5 mm discs on BOTH inner and outer (dual facing).
Reason: FoM (P·Φ) dominates; Ø3 area is (3/20)^2 ≈ 2.25% → voltage collapses despite same P.
Topology: outer+inner magnet drums locked as one rotor; coil cylinder fixed stator.

Baseline @ 120 RPM (P=8, gap=2 mm, 26 AWG):
  Voc_rms ≈ 4.139 V, Voc_peak ≈ 5.853 V
  f = 8.0 Hz, N≈501, R≈49.81 Ω
  LED current est ≈ 17.04 mA (100 Ω series, 2 V LED, after naive bridge)

Ø3 inner same conditions @ 120 RPM:
  Voc_rms ≈ 0.093 V, I_led ≈ 0.0 mA
